# PR #202 - Preserve video-only route points in Quick Recap

- **Source head:** `096c5308539461cb35fc95a82dd850ebc18398e3`
- **Scope:** Replaces Quick Recap's image-only runtime candidate projection with a visual-media projection: `runtimeVisualCandidates()` keeps every non-soundtrack asset (reusing `journeyModel`'s `isVisualMediaAsset`/`isSoundtrackAsset` classification), video digests are emitted with `mediaType: "video"` plus an explicit analysis-pending `intrinsic.durationMs` (`QUICK_RECAP_PENDING_VIDEO_DURATION_MS`, reusing Full Playback's `PLAYBACK_PACING.videoMs`), and the fallback reason `no-photos` becomes `no-visual-media`. `autoEditPlan.ts` is untouched.
- **User-visible change:** A route point whose only media is a video keeps its Quick Recap chapter instead of vanishing from the recap route, and a video-only Journey now plays a Quick Recap instead of silently falling back to Full Playback. The fallback notice now reads 照片或视频 and only appears when a Journey really has no visual media.
- **Review fixes:** The duration fallback is load-bearing, not cosmetic: `autoEditPlan`'s video eligibility rule rejects an absent, non-finite, or non-positive duration, so an empty `intrinsic` would have deleted the route point's only chapter — the very regression this fixes. Two further review findings were then corrected. First, the cover projection relocated the explicit cover to the first route point unconditionally, so a cover that was its owning point's only visual media emptied that chapter and deleted the route point; the cover now moves only when its owner keeps at least one other visual asset (or when it is journey-scoped, or already at the first point), it is never duplicated, and cover-first source ordering applies only to a cover that actually moved, so a cover left in place cannot be hoisted ahead of earlier chapters. This bug pre-existed for image covers and video covers merely inherited it, so both are now covered by tests. Second, `stableCandidateSort` carried no media-type term, so a video that happened to be uploaded before the photos at the same route point became that point's mandatory representative and pushed the photos into the optional fill, where a tight budget cut them — seven points each holding a video then a photo selected seven videos and one photo under the 45s target. An image now outranks a video on an otherwise equal tie, ranked strictly below the pinned/cover hard signals so a cover or pinned video still wins by user intent and a video-only point still gets its video. That single comparison is the only deliberate change to `autoEditPlan.ts`; no existing fixture depended on a video winning a tie.
- **Follow-up:** Phase 2 of #195 remains: measured video duration, bounded highlight segments, and a trim-aware playback contract. Phase 1 deliberately keeps clips whole — the planner's `trim.inMs` is always 0 and the director holds a video step until the media element's real `ended` event, so `trim.outMs` is a planning budget only and no trim is wired into `<video>`. Family search (#198) found no other image-only candidate projection: `journeyPlaybackPlan.ts` and `journeyKeepsake.ts` already classify video, and `JourneyPlaybackOverlay.tsx`'s `image/` checks are decode gating (a video-only chapter correctly releases the hold), not topology.
