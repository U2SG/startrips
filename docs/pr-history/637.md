# PR #637 - Add opt-in real-time day/night to the particle globe

- **Source head:** `ac66917d270e461805729913dfb96d26b23b9cec`
- **Scope:** `?dayNight=1` turns on a real-time day/night terminator, and `?sunTime=<ISO>` pins its clock. `src/scene/sunPosition.ts` computes the subsolar point (declination plus the equation of time) and turns it into a direction through `latLonToVector3`. A `dayNight` material option adds `uSunDirection` and `uDayNightStrength`, compiled only when the flag is on, to the base land layer and the spatial-LOD refinement layer. The night side is darkened to a 0.60 floor, which keeps the faintest refinement particle above the 0.015 discard threshold. The atmosphere rim is weighted toward the day side without exceeding its current peak, and coastlines follow the night amount at the view centre. The sun is refreshed every 60 s, or frozen under reduced motion or `sunTime`. Day/night is off in Surface Earth. Its strength eases out over three wheel notches of log zoom (1.89 to 2.71), and an un-eased cap holds it at exactly 0 from local progress 0.44, before the Earth Dive blend at 0.45. No textures, geometries, render targets or passes are added.
- **User-visible change:** None by default. With `?dayNight=1`, the particle globe shows the current night side as a soft darkening.
- **Review fixes:** The Earth Dive overlap hint is not used, because it is already true during the regional prewarm. The zoom fade reads the continuous canonical zoom instead of the published `localProgress`, which used to pop the strength from 1 to 0.51 when the `local` band opened at zoom 2.63. Wheel zoom lands a whole notch at once (zoom times `e^0.12`, about +0.33 at zoom 2.55), so the fade was widened to three notches in log zoom and is eased. The un-eased cap binds only when input outruns the easing. Continuity tests are bounded by the slope times 2ε, and a separate test checks the notch span.
- **Follow-up:** Coastlines use a single view-centre factor, which is approximate at global zoom. Night-side visited-imprint glow, and making this the default, are out of scope.
- **Validation:** Locally: `pnpm typecheck`, `git diff --check` and the ledger validators. Unit tests, build and browser QA run on GitHub Actions; exact Source CI and exact final-head CI are authoritative. Visual feel-check is pending reviewer capture.
