# PR #677 - Pull the 3D Journey Book camera back less and more gently while a page turns

- **Source head:** `c87e3fd02790d4f7bd52b390dd64e5a8b4b78586`
- **Scope:** `BOOK_CAMERA_TILT` 20° → 15°. `MAX_PULLBACK = 0.08` caps the live frame at 8% taller than the rest frame (`pullBackTopLimit`, `cappedFlightFrameTop`). `stepFrameFollow` is an exact critically damped camera follow (0.25 s) on real elapsed time, clamped between the rest frame and the cap, snapping to the rest frame when idle. An automatic turn leads the camera to its peak frame (`autoTurnFrameTop`, progress-scheduled `autoTurnFloorTop`); `turnPeakSheetTop` is exact for a rigid board and measured once on a real quick_flipbook sheet for paper. Drags follow the measured sheet; reduced motion snaps. The onFrame settled flag also waits for the camera to land. `JourneyBook3d` feeds a DEV-only per-frame QA probe; `scripts/qa-journey-book-3d.mjs` gains check 6 (automatic turns, every frame) and runs the desktop and phone sessions in separate browsers.
- **User-visible change:** Turning a page pulls the camera back at most 8% (the book shrinks about 7% mid-turn, down from about 20%). The camera eases out and back softly instead of tracking the sheet. A rigid cover standing upright briefly passes the canvas top by up to about 6.6% of the stage, an owner decision. The page-block strip under the near edge is about 24% thinner.
