# PR #727 - Route one sentence from a command bar to proposal cards

- **Source head:** `dc15074e5b41201cfd3ca0f12135e4e9c0fa557b`
- **Scope:** `POST /api/intents/route` reads one sentence in two stages (stage 1 `read`: sentence + offered intents only; stage 2 re-checks the chosen intent's own permission and calls its existing reader: share / share-photos-filter, route-order on the draft points, navigate over Journey titles/dates, create-journey and globe-focus without a model call), one sentence per Atlas at a time, per-intent length limits; `/capabilities` reports `router:{configured, intents}`. Desktop `CommandBar` (header trigger, ⌘K/Ctrl+K, also inside an open Composer/share dialog) answers with one `ProposalCard` (确认/修改/取消, latency states, 停止, 65 s cap). The share dialog's sentence form and the Composer's route-order form are removed; the Composer's one-sentence Journey entry hides only when the bar handles create-journey. The QA harness answers the capabilities read as unconfigured by default.
- **User-visible change:** On desktop, with an intent provider, one sentence can share Journeys (link created on 确认), share only photos without people, reorder a draft route, start a Journey, open a Journey or enter globe focus. Without a provider nothing changes.
- **Review fixes:** Codex: `/capabilities` now reports the router (the bar would otherwise never render); Escape cannot dismiss a card while its share link is being created. CI: the photo-filter card's batch loop survives a StrictMode remount (progress and skip list kept); no-API QA suites get a default capabilities answer.
- **Follow-up:** Mobile dock, one-sentence photo assignment and Everyday intents, and removal of now-unused client helpers come in later PRs. No browser QA yet for the create-journey and navigate confirm paths.
- **Validation:** Local `pnpm typecheck`, `git diff --check`, `node --check`. CI run 39909674669 on the Source head (rebased on main 44388ff8): `core`, `keepsake-render` and all eight browser-qa shards passed.
