# PR #669 - Name the decode wait in Playback after a short grace

- **Source head:** `407292a60fb99930175d2d8e43ac9999f95c11fe`
- **Scope:** `JourneyPlaybackOverlay` starts a 1.5 s grace when `playbackHoldReason` becomes `decode` and, if the hold outlives it, shows `正在准备下一张照片` in the existing `journey-playback__status` pill (with `data-playback-decode-wait` for QA); the notice clears when the hold lifts. `playbackStatusLine` in `playbackMediaPresentation.ts` is the pure rule (the shell's `statusMessage` wins; video and trim holds stay quiet). The grace timer is keyed by `playbackDecodeWaitKey(holdReason, stepIndex, heldAssetId)`, where the hold effect publishes the asset it waits on, so Next, Back or a scrub to another decoding image restarts the grace instead of inheriting the previous image's elapsed wait. Both helpers are unit-tested. No timing, director or Ken Burns change.
- **User-visible change:** While Playback waits on a large original to download and decode, the viewer sees what the wait is instead of a frame that stops moving.
- **Review fixes:** Codex P2 (the grace timer depended only on `holdReason`, so a move between two decoding images inherited the first image's elapsed grace): the timer is now keyed on the beat and the held asset.
