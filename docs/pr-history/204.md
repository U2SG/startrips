# PR #204 - Restore mobile Story media playback affordance

- **Source head:** `1084faae2f778e2a70f1c84216958dfef4bcad6d`
- **Scope:** Restores the mobile Story Viewer play/pause affordance for multi-media stories and aligns Story autoplay with mixed image/video completion, browser user-activation constraints, signed-read ownership, native video controls, stall recovery, fullscreen, swipe ownership, and two-layer media transitions. The final implementation uses persistent inline/fullscreen stage video nodes, prefetches the first future video read, gates autoplay until the candidate URL is ready, and reuses the same authorized node as a video becomes incoming and then settled.
- **User-visible change:** Mobile Story Viewer users can start or pause sequence playback without entering Manage mode. Images retain timed progression; videos play to completion, recover from rejected play or non-terminal stalls, stop Story autoplay when paused through native controls, and do not get silently skipped when reached later in the sequence. Fullscreen playback uses the video node authorized by the fullscreen-entry gesture, and swiping remains attached to the visible settled media rather than hidden priming media.
