# PR #653 - Bring Story and Playback controls into the control family

- **Source head:** `1162178ffc4d9be8819e7d2168bdb11a0121fa16`
- **Scope:** CSS only, no TSX. Journey Story is portaled to `<body>`, where `--atlas-*` never resolves, so its controls now read the `:root` `--control-*` tokens from their own selectors in `living-atlas.css`, `story-experience.css` and `story-notes.css`. The `IconActionButton` baseline scope in `controls.css` adds `.journey-story-backdrop` and `.journey-playback`; Playback renders inside `.living-atlas` and was already covered, and is named so it keeps the baseline wherever it mounts. Story: the mobile close is the family icon circle and still widens into a status pill only during upload, delete or save; desktop header, media-nav and footer controls are quiet 44px pills and circles; set cover and delete are the 44px baseline circles on the stage's dense glass with ink instead of 40px acid squares; the overview and select toggles, quick-recap choices, add media, retry, soundtrack, placement, footer and both delete confirmations are secondary pills; pressed and selected states (media nav, immersive nav, the two toggles and the mobile chapter chips, now `--radius-tile` tiles) use `--control-select`; destructive actions are text-forward in `--control-danger`; the coordinate inputs take the field look without spinners and stay `type=number`; the note textareas take `--radius-field`. A closing block in the Story section adds the 2px focus ring (replacing the desktop 1px starlight ring and the per-control border recipes), the shared danger tint, scale on press for pills and circles, a tint for rows and fine-pointer hover. Playback: the transport is one capsule of outlined icon circles with a cyan paused state; the close, return-location and selection-summary pills read the tokens; the tempo select takes the `.st-select` look (sans, chevron, dark popup, inset under 30px for the 64px compact column); the dense navigator gets 44px buttons with a trimmed inset, so its 156 x 48 outline is unchanged; the misplaced reduced-motion tempo padding is removed; a closing block adds the focus ring, press and hover. Both closes keep their idle-fade `opacity` transition.
- **User-visible change:** Story and Playback buttons match the rest of the Atlas: round icon buttons, dark-glass secondary pills, cyan for pressed and selected states, a muted danger tint for destructive actions, one 2px acid focus ring and a small press response. Playback's controls sit in one rounded capsule with a sans tempo picker. Desktop Story's quiet controls use the family muted and ink colours instead of the starlight palette, and the mobile active chapter chip is cyan instead of acid. No layout, gesture or motion changes.
- **Review fixes:** None.
