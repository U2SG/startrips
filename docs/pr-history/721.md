# PR #721 - Cover the Journey Book with its photo, or a starry sky

- **Source head:** `0f8e8d1305e2fbf67b6368c1151217ebbb0f458a`
- **Scope:** New `src/journey/journeyBookCover.ts` chooses the front cover: the Journey's chosen cover asset when it is in the payload and visual, else the first visual asset; with none, a seeded starry sky (#0b1222, globe-cyan stars, gold route #d9b97a). The 2D book (`JourneyBook.tsx`, `journey-book.css`) and the 3D painter draw a full-bleed photo with a solid night title band (`COVER_TITLE_BAND`, 5%–29% of the cover, #0b1222 at 0.8, no gradient) behind title and dates, and a light-on-dark cover route; the tipped-in plate and the blind mark are removed on photo covers, the mark is gold on the starry cover, and the sky is always painted under the photo. Spine and back use the night base.
- **User-visible change:** Journey Book and 3D Journey Book covers show the Journey's photo across the whole cover, or a starry sky when there is no photo, instead of the grey-green cloth.
- **Review fixes:** Codex: a video cover without a poster no longer hides the sky behind the video frame's solid background. Owner review of CI screenshots: a gradient head scrim (0.38, then 0.55) left the title unreadable on pale photos under the 3D raking light; it is replaced by the solid title band, starting below a thin photo strip so the 3D cover-edge probe still reads the photo.
- **Follow-up:** CI fixtures render CJK glyphs in the 3D painter's serif stack as tofu (CI installs Noto Sans SC only); real devices use system fonts. The wide groove under the gold line on the starry 3D cover is unchanged.
- **Validation:** Local `pnpm typecheck`, `git diff --check`, `node --check scripts/qa-journey-book-3d.mjs`. CI run 39887601898 on the Source head (rebased on main bf72906e): `core`, `keepsake-render` and all eight browser-qa shards passed; owner approved the 3D cover screenshot.
