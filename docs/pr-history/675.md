# PR #675 - Fill Quick Recap vias greedily once the Stops are booked

- **Source head:** `b7b0dbcc069cbbe1c409c9117de97c998923952a`
- **Scope:** `buildDeterministicQuickRecapPlan` books only Stops as the mandatory floor and fills transit vias greedily in route order, admitting a via whole (representative plus camera beat) when it fits and skipping it whole otherwise. A pinned or cover asset on a via is a hard selection and is never traded for budget. Legs are priced from the previous retained chapter through the new cumulative `routeDistanceFromStart` in `QuickRecapRouteGeometryV1` (filled by `quickRecapRouteGeometry`), so a via retained after a skipped one books the leg Full Playback actually flies; the candidate-chain `angularDistanceFromPrevious` remains the fallback. Budget checks and chapter emission share one pricing. `prepareQuickRecapPlaybackResult` takes `tempoFallback`: when the requested tempo cannot fit the Stops, or fits them without a single photographed via, it re-plans at the fallback tempo and keeps that plan when it brings a via in; the shell passes `fast`. `useJourneyPlaybackDirector` gains `initialTempo`, seeded by the overlay from `quickRecapPlan.tempo`. `validateAutoEditPlanV1`'s coverage rule exempts transit vias (their omission is declared by the omission ledger and a skip the builder would not make is still a selection mismatch). The final-acceptance QA's over-budget Journey grows from ten to sixteen intercontinental Stops so it stays over budget at both tempos.
- **User-visible change:** Quick Recap no longer refuses a long Journey: a 25-point, 66-media Journey that always answered "放不下所有必要的旅程点" now plays a 45 s recap that keeps every Stop and, at the fast tempo the planner falls back to, carries the photographed transit vias (Bryce Canyon, Zion, Death Valley, Stanford). A Journey whose standard plan already fits with a via keeps the standard tempo. The refusal remains only when the Stops alone exceed the budget at both tempos.
- **Review fixes:** Codex P1 (validator rejected a via skipped for budget): coverage rule exempts transit vias; thread resolved by the reviewer. Codex P1 (a pinned/cover via asset could be deleted by the fit loop): hard selections are skipped by the loop; new test keeps the pin at an over-full target. Codex P2 (a retained via's leg was priced from the skipped candidate): cumulative route distances price legs from the previous retained chapter; new test books travel(0.6 rad) rather than travel(0.1 rad) after a skip. Earlier re-forms: the owner-approved tempo fallback and the final-acceptance fixture.
- **Follow-up:** The recap still budgets each Stop's arrival and leg before any media; a long Journey of many empty Stops spends most of 45 s on arrivals. Whether empty Stops should cost less in a recap is a product decision not taken here.
