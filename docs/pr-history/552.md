# PR #552 - Renew fullscreen video reads without losing the frame

- **Source head:** `2588db031cad0a76fa6ad03e4cd8fdc31bf726f9`
- **Scope:** Let a settled paused fullscreen video renew its signed read in the visible stage while keeping its decoded frame, seek position, native settings, and one presented video transport. An expired old-URL Range failure after a native seek keeps the last painted frame and the requested seek time behind Retry; Retry reloads bytes even on the same URL; Close during renewed-byte decode hands the held frame and time back to inline Story. Story autoplay keeps its video read protected as on `main`.
- **User-visible change:** A paused fullscreen video no longer drops to a blank or first frame when its access expires or is renewed. Seeking into unfetched bytes after expiry shows the last picture with Retry, and Retry resumes at the requested position.
- **Review fixes:** The expired-range browser check compared floored light from the held canvas bitmap with a screenshot; the same paused frame measures about 2.4x brighter sampled from that canvas than from its video, so a correctly shown frame failed at energy ratio 0.418. The screen check against a held bitmap now compares unfloored light. Independent review then found that the continuity anchor was taken after the error; QA now captures the screen while the expired Range is still withheld and fails the error, Retry-read and Retry-byte steps unless each screen matches that pre-error picture (CI: aligned 0.997, energy 0.957). Review also found that the Story-autoplay read guard had been dropped; it is restored so only a viewer-paused clip takes the held-frame renewal path. Three diagnostic tracing commits were dropped.
- **Follow-up:** Renewal still triggers three concurrent requests for the renewed URL (the presented video, the inactive stage's hidden handoff video, and its detached first-frame preparation), as inline renewal from #550 already does; reducing the inactive stage's preload needs its own change that keeps Close handoff intact. Continue #489 investigation for black-frame reports outside this Story renewal path.
