# PR #552 - Renew fullscreen video reads without losing the frame

- **Source head:** `fcb33af7061945412684034bdbb8ddc332cab90e`
- **Scope:** Let a settled paused fullscreen video renew its signed read in the visible stage while keeping its decoded frame, seek position, native settings, and one video transport. An expired old-URL Range failure after a native seek keeps the last painted frame and the requested seek time behind Retry; Retry reloads bytes even on the same URL; Close during renewed-byte decode hands the held frame and time back to inline Story.
- **User-visible change:** A paused fullscreen video no longer drops to a blank or first frame when its access expires or is renewed. Seeking into unfetched bytes after expiry shows the last picture with Retry, and Retry resumes at the requested position.
- **Review fixes:** The expired-range browser check compared floored light from the held canvas bitmap with a screenshot; the same paused frame measures about 2.4x brighter sampled from that canvas than from its video, so a correctly shown frame failed at energy ratio 0.418. The screen check against a held bitmap now compares unfloored light, and Retry steps must match the frame anchored when the error appeared instead of the pre-seek picture. Three diagnostic tracing commits were dropped.
- **Follow-up:** Continue #489 investigation for black-frame reports outside this Story renewal path.
