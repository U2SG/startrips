# PR #306 - Require explicit Full Playback after Quick Recap overflow

- **Source head:** `dc10e19e8e5e93df44098be9939d9f6939bb95b7`
- **Scope:** ST-011 only. Changes the Living Atlas handling of Quick Recap `over-budget` results so they stop at an explicit choice surface instead of silently transitioning to Full Playback. The Quick Recap planner, timing constants, selected-content rules, Home/session playback topology, same-asset preview layering and the `no-visual-media` fallback remain unchanged.
- **User-visible change:** When required Journey content cannot fit the Quick Recap target, the user sees `当前回顾时长放不下所有必要的旅程点。` and an explicit accessible `完整播放` action. Full Playback does not mount or claim playback-entry/soundtrack preparation until that action is chosen; short and medium Quick Recaps continue through the existing path.
- **Review fixes:** Closed both review findings on the final Source head. The explicit `完整播放` action now receives keyboard focus after the Quick Recap button is replaced by the over-budget result, and same-ID Journey edits invalidate the stored over-budget choice through the Journey revision so a newly eligible recap can be requested again. Final-acceptance QA activates Quick Recap from the keyboard, verifies the focused explicit action while no Playback is mounted, then verifies that choosing it starts Full Playback for the same long intercontinental Journey.
- **Follow-up:** V1 deliberately does not add automatic compression or silently start Full Playback. Future adaptive compression, if desired, needs a separate product rule for what content or dwell may be reduced. `no-visual-media` remains outside ST-011 and keeps its existing fallback behavior.
