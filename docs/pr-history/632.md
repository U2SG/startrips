# PR #632 - Wire Atlas wordmark brand states to entry, mutations and arrivals

- **Source head:** `d85604acb9686b74247ea0bbfddd2de3e7a6b45d`
- **Scope:** The Atlas wordmark now receives a resolved V1 brand state: `travel` once on the first shell paint, `waiting` while a composer save/media retry or a delete/restore is in flight, and `arrived` once after a create/edit save or restore. The pure resolver, the token-derived durations and the one-shot moment consumption live in `src/journey/atlasBrandState.ts`. `JourneyComposer` reports its existing `saving` flag through an optional `onMutationPendingChange`. Keyframes, geometry, colours and assets are unchanged.
- **User-visible change:** The top-left Startrips wordmark enters once when the Atlas opens, breathes while a Journey is saved, deleted or restored, and settles with one arrive motion after a successful save or restore. It stays still under reduced motion and during selection, scrubbing and media switching.
- **Review fixes:** Owner follow-up: a mutation that starts during the opening `travel` now spends it, so the wordmark goes `waiting` → `arrived` (success) or `rest` (failure) and never replays `travel`. Unit tests cover both sequences.
- **Follow-up:** None.
- **Validation:** Locally: `pnpm typecheck`, `git diff --check` and the ledger validators. Authoritative: GitHub CI (vitest, including `atlasBrandState.test.ts`, browser QA and the build). Browser feel-check delegated to the reviewer.
