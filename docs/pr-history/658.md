# PR #658 - Let the cover's goat pull the 3D Journey Book open

- **Source head:** `dbcd713ee882584ab5cb4ec23be319aa59f34095`
- **Scope:** A brand moment on the 3D Journey Book's closed front cover. The embossed v12 goat lifts into a live SVG goat. It reuses `StartripsGoatRig` and `applyPose`, now exported from `StartripsSignatureMotion.tsx`, with one additive optional pose param `rootRotateDeg` that leans the goat about its hind hooves. The cover is repainted without the goat in both the colour face and the material map, and only the star stays stamped (`goatAway`, part of the cover's repaint signature). `coverMarkFrame` in the painter places both the emboss and the live goat. The goat's articulation is a new keyframe clip (`src/brand/startripsPullClip.ts`), kept outside `STARTRIPS_SIGNATURE_CLIPS` so the existing clips are unchanged. `journeyBookGoatPull.ts` holds the pure timeline-to-cover progress and staging, plus eligibility, the mark hit test and the once-per-device played list (localStorage, with an in-session fallback). The sequence is wake 0–0.4 s, walk to 1.2 s, then a 4° tug that drops back shut and a 10° tug that drops back to 3°. From 2.4 s it heaves to 0.55 by 3.2 s, leaving at quick_flipbook's turn rate, and is then handed to the book's own turn. The goat grows from its stamp to a tenth of the cover's width while waking, steps down into the strip below the tipped-in plate and walks it to the fore-edge, lets go at 2.65 s, hops off and fades from release to 3.4 s. Its clock advances by the scene's clamped frame step (at most 40 ms), as the book's own turns do. `journeyBookGoatPullStage.ts` runs it inside the scene's single frame loop (`scene.drive`). Each frame, the cover angle (`dragTo(p, bendForward)`), the pose and the place come from the deformed cover mesh (`coverPoint`, `coverRestPoint`). `requestRender` is a no-op inside a frame. Interrupts: window-capture pointerdown, wheel or keydown, or a turn command, takes the goat off screen inside that input's own handler. The cover is then let down from its angle (`layDown`), caught by the press's drag (`takeEdge(true)`), or completed if it is past upright. `turnTo` no longer snaps a lifted edge.
- **User-visible change:** On a Journey's closed 3D book, after about 900 ms without input, once per Journey per device, the goat climbs out of the cover's emboss, walks to the fore-edge, tugs the cover twice and heaves it open onto the first spread, then hops off and fades. A tap on the cover's goat mark replays it. Any input takes over immediately. Reduced motion never plays it. A hover-lifted page edge now turns from where it is on an arrow key, footer step or contents jump.
