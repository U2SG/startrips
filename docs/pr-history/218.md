# PR #218 - Sign guest media reads with a grant-capped lifetime

- **Source head:** `d629a6e06f8d7287e4b344d21aac6b003a72c405`
- **Scope:** #200 phase C. New `GET /api/shared/assets/:assetId/read-url` on the guest bearer path, backed by a new `server/repositories/shared-media-repository.ts`: `resolveSharedMediaRead` re-derives grant membership from the asset's current `journey_id` inside one `repeatable read`, `read only` transaction whose first statement re-evaluates the grant, comparing `journeys.atlas_id` against the grant's atlas and excluding a journey inside its deletion grace window, and returns the grant's expiry as an absolute instant. `signSharedMediaRead` in `server/routes/shares.ts` derives `min(share ceiling, owner ceiling, remaining lifetime)` from the clock immediately before the presign, refuses below one second, and refuses a signature whose returned expiry reaches past the grant deadline. New `SHARE_MEDIA_READ_URL_EXPIRES_IN_SECONDS` config (15-600, default 90) wired through `.env.example`, `deploy/env.example`, `deploy/compose.yaml` and documented in `deploy/README.md`. No schema change, so no migration and no regenerated `server/db/auth-schema.ts`; no client file touched, because the guest viewer is phase D.
- **User-visible change:** None yet. The endpoint exists and is tested but has no caller until the phase D guest viewer; an owner's own media reads are unchanged.
- **Review fixes:** Codex thread 3934912811 (P1) was verified against the code and accepted: the resolver's default `now` was captured before `db.transaction` acquired a connection, and the duration it returned was then counted again from the storage adapter's later signing clock, so a request stalled on a saturated pool could receive a full remaining lifetime after the grant had expired. Fixed in `d629a6e` by returning `grantExpiresAt` as an instant and deriving the lifetime at signing time, plus a post-condition on what the adapter returned; answered in reply 3934963677 with two new cases, `derives the TTL from the signing clock, not from when the request started` and `refuses a signature that would reach past the grant deadline`.
- **Follow-up:** The residual window after a revoke is unchanged and unfixable by a read - a presigned URL already in a guest's hands works until it expires, now bounded at 90 s by default, and `deploy/README.md` says so. The client-side half of the new time dimension is recorded as a Known gap in `docs/cross-feature-assumption-audit.md`: `LivingAtlasApp.tsx`, `JourneyStory.tsx` and `soundtrackReadCache.ts` all encode a fixed refresh margin that assumes a lifetime of minutes, and none may be reused by the phase D viewer without deriving its margin from the returned `expiresAt`. The rate limiting #200 asks for is deliberately absent: ST-002's acceptance does not require it and #217 has just removed the global limiter.
- **Validation:** `pnpm typecheck`, `git diff --check` and `node scripts/pr-history.mjs validate-all` / `validate-pr` locally, captured under `.agent-artifacts/st-002/`. Every test, migration and browser lane is delegated to CI by product-owner rule; nothing ran against a local database, browser or object store. Object storage is unconfigured in CI, so the `guest media read` integration block asserts the authorization decision and the two unavailable shapes through the real route, and drives the signing step through an injected adapter for the lifetime cases; the uninjected happy path stops at a truthful `STORAGE_UNAVAILABLE` 503 rather than a fabricated URL. `server/repositories/shared-media-repository.test.ts` sweeps every remaining lifetime from 1 to 600 s and asserts the issued TTL never reaches past the deadline. CI on the submitted head is authoritative.
