# PR #633 - fix(mobile): native-feeling baseline for touch devices

- **Source head:** `03411219e14f18158ae86fcff8f0c7d972380636`
- **Scope:** Platform-layer touch fixes, with no redesign:
  - All 55 ungated `:hover` selectors now sit behind `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`. Two rules stay exempt: the autofill workaround and the reduced-motion reset.
  - A zero-specificity `:where()` baseline gives controls `touch-action: manipulation` and `user-select: none`, so gesture surfaces keep their own touch-action.
  - Text fields have a 16px floor on coarse pointers. The title and legacy year fields opt out.
  - `overscroll-behavior: none` is set on `html, body`.
  - The last `100vh` sites now use `svh` or `dvh`.
  - The viewport sets `viewport-fit=cover`, and every edge-touching surface that lacked safe-area offsets now has them. That includes the Playback controls, the expanded Story handle and header, the phone Composer, the Mobile V2 chrome, sheets and cards, Share, the account dock, the toasts and the pick hint.
  - `theme-color` is set per scheme to `--archive-bg #020706`.
  - A new `mobile-native` browser QA suite runs in the `labels-recovery` shard.
- **User-visible change:** On phones, tapped controls no longer keep their hover style. Taps fire without a double-tap delay, and long-pressing a control no longer selects its label. Focusing a field no longer zooms the page, and the root no longer pull-to-refreshes. On notched iPhones the app paints edge to edge with controls kept clear of the notch and home indicator, and the status bar matches the app.
- **Review fixes:** The root holds only vertical overscroll (`overscroll-behavior-y`), so horizontal back/forward history swipes keep working; the QA asserts the X axis stays `auto`. The auth wordmark and caption take the horizontal safe-area insets in landscape. None.
- **Follow-up:** These still need a real device: sticky hover, tap delay, safe areas in portrait and landscape, the software keyboard, and the status bar colour.
- **Validation:** Local checks were `pnpm typecheck`, `git diff --check`, `node --check scripts/qa-mobile-native.mjs`, and `node --test` on the two CI-tooling suite-count tests. Exact Source CI 37197075832 passed quick-checks, core, keepsake-render and every browser-qa lane, including `mobile-native`. This final commit changes only this ledger.
